Research Writing

Clear, structured and evidence-based research support for academic, policy and professional work.

The focus is on rigorous research development: literature synthesis, methodology, empirical analysis, interpretation and revision—not on making unsupported claims look convincing.

What I do

Research Support

01

Research Development

Refining topics, questions, objectives, scope and the analytical structure of a project.

02

Literature Synthesis

Organising evidence around themes, debates, gaps and theoretical or empirical relevance.

03

Empirical Analysis

Integrating data work and econometric results into a coherent analytical narrative.

04

Policy Interpretation

Connecting findings to realistic economic implications while keeping conclusions proportionate to the evidence.
