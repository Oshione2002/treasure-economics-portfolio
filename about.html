How I work

My Approach

Evidence-based

I start with credible sources, transparent assumptions and a research question that can be answered with evidence.

Context-driven

Economic results make more sense when they are interpreted in the institutional and policy context that produced them.

Methodical

Data preparation, model choice, diagnostics and interpretation are treated as connected parts of the same research process.

Clear communication

Technical work is translated into readable explanations without hiding uncertainty or overstating what the evidence shows.
